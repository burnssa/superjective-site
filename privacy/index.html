What you need to know

Superjective is committed to transparency in how we handle your data. We help users compare responses from different large language models (LLMs) while contributing to AI research. We never sell your personal information and only collect what's necessary to provide our service. Anonymous comparison data may be shared with research partners solely to create better, safer AI models. We use Auth0 for secure authentication and PostHog for basic analytics. Please don't submit personal information in your prompts—our service works best when you keep sensitive data private.

What information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your email address and any optional profile information you choose to provide. Passwords are managed securely through Auth0.
  • Subscription details: For paid tiers, we collect billing information necessary to process your payment through our payment processor.
  • Content you submit: The prompts you enter and your feedback on LLM responses when using our comparison tools.
  • Communications: Information you provide when contacting us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: How you interact with our service, including features used and actions taken.
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, and device type.
  • General location: Country and region based on your IP address.

Information from third parties

  • Authentication data: When you sign in using Auth0, we receive basic profile information necessary to create and manage your account.

Information we don't collect

To protect your privacy, we explicitly do NOT collect:

  • Tracking cookies or advertising identifiers
  • Cross-site browsing history
  • Third-party analytics data
  • Location data beyond general timezone
  • Any data from social media plugins

How we use your information

Core service functionality

  • Providing access to our LLM comparison tools
  • Managing your account and subscription
  • Processing payments for paid subscriptions
  • Maintaining and improving our services
  • Communicating important service updates

Analytics and improvement

  • Understanding how users interact with our platform
  • Identifying technical issues and improvement opportunities
  • Analyzing feature usage
  • Resolving bugs and performance issues

Research and AI advancement

We're passionate about improving AI safety and capabilities. With appropriate safeguards:

  • We analyze anonymous comparison interaction data to identify patterns in LLM performance
  • We use aggregated insights to develop better evaluation methodologies
  • We may contribute anonymized data to help create safer, more effective AI models

Data retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. We periodically review our data retention practices and delete data that is no longer needed.

When we share your information

Service providers

We share limited information with essential service providers:

  • Auth0 (Okta, Inc.): For secure authentication
  • PostHog: For understanding platform usage
  • Stripe (or similar): For payment processing
  • Cloud infrastructure providers: For hosting our service

These providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only to provide services to us.

LLM providers

When you submit prompts through our platform, they are sent to third-party LLM providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) to generate responses. We recommend:

  • Not including personal information in prompts
  • Reviewing the privacy policies of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google
  • Understanding that we cannot control how LLM providers process prompts once submitted

Research purposes

We may share anonymous comparison data with academic institutions or research organizations to improve AI safety and capabilities. This data:

  • Never includes personally identifiable information
  • Is aggregated across many users
  • Cannot be traced back to individual users

Legal requirements

We may disclose information when we believe in good faith that it's necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal process
  • Protect the safety of any person
  • Prevent fraud or security issues
  • Protect our legal rights

Our Cookie-Free Approach

We don't use tracking cookies

Superjective is committed to your privacy. We do not use any third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that follow you across the web.

Essential cookies only

  • Authentication cookies: These essential cookies keep you logged in securely via Auth0
  • No tracking cookies: We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking
  • No advertising cookies: We don't participate in ad networks or retargeting

How we measure usage without cookies

  • Server-side analytics: We analyze usage patterns on our servers without tracking you
  • Privacy-preserving metrics: We use aggregated, anonymous data that can't identify individuals
  • Session-based insights: Temporary session data that's deleted when you close your browser

Your privacy is protected by design

  • All analytics are anonymous and aggregated
  • No personal data is ever sold or shared with advertisers
  • You can use our service with ad blockers enabled
  • No cross-site tracking is possible

How we protect your data

Security measures

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication through Auth0
  • Regular security reviews

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data breach response

If we become aware of a security breach that compromises your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

Important precautions

  • Never submit personally identifiable information in your prompts
  • Avoid entering sensitive data like SSNs, credit card numbers, or health information
  • Remember that prompts are sent to third-party LLM providers

Your privacy rights
