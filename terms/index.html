What Superjective is and how it works

Superjective is a platform that enables users to compare responses from different Large Language Models (LLMs) through a blind comparison workflow. The platform allows you to submit prompts, view anonymized responses from various LLMs, provide feedback on these responses, and then see which models generated which responses. The platform may be accessed via web, mobile, or voice interfaces.

The platform is designed to help you make informed decisions about which LLM solutions might best suit your needs, while simultaneously contributing to AI research through your interactions and feedback. Your anonymous comparison data helps inform the creation and selection of better AI models.
