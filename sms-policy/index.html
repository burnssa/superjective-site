Opt-In Process

You explicitly opt-in to SMS messages by:

  • Calling our advice line and confirming your phone number via verification code
  • Adding and verifying your phone number in your account settings at superjective.ai

By completing either verification process, you agree to receive SMS messages from Superjective. You must verify ownership of the phone number before receiving messages.

You will receive one SMS message per advice request, typically within 2-3 minutes of your phone call. Messages contain a secure link to view your AI comparison results and do not include the actual AI responses for privacy and message length reasons.

Opt-Out Instructions

You can opt-out of SMS messages at any time by:

  • Reply STOP to any SMS message from Superjective

  • Emailing support@superjective.ai with your opt-out request

Once you opt-out, you will no longer receive SMS messages. You can opt back in by re-verifying your phone number through your account settings.
